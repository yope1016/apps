What the numbers are based on

Daily food, transport and activity ranges, plus hotel prices by city and season, are based on 2026 price checks published by travel-cost guides (see sources below) and adjusted by city. Kyoto's lodging tax uses the city's official rates from March 2026. These are planning estimates, not quotes — actual prices change by date, hotel and how early you book.

Why is Kyoto lodging tax so high on luxury stays?

Since March 1, 2026, Kyoto charges ¥200 to ¥10,000 per person, per night, depending on the room price: under ¥6,000 → ¥200; ¥6,000–19,999 → ¥400; ¥20,000–49,999 → ¥1,000; ¥50,000–99,999 → ¥4,000; ¥100,000 and up → ¥10,000. Tokyo, Osaka and several other places charge smaller amounts, which this calculator approximates.

Is a nationwide rail pass worth it?

Add up the train journeys you plan to take. If they cost more than the pass for the same number of days, the pass may pay off. Many short trips (for example only Tokyo, Kyoto and Osaka) often cost less as single tickets. Always compare with the official pass price before buying.

Will I get my tax back?

From November 1, 2026, Japan switched to a refund system: you pay the full price including tax at the shop, then get the tax back after customs confirms you're taking the goods out of Japan.

At the airport: the new steps

  1. Keep your goods and receipts with youDon't check in that luggage yet. If even one item from a receipt is missing, the whole receipt loses the refund.
  2. Scan your passport at a tax-free kioskLook for the tax-free procedure terminals before security. At major airports you can also do this online via Visit Japan Web in marked areas.
  3. Green = done, red = inspectionA red result means you show your goods at the customs inspection area. Finish this before boarding.
  4. Get your refund from the shopThe store (or a refund company it uses) pays you back after customs confirms the export. Ask at the till how and when they refund — it varies by shop.

Japan's standard consumption tax is 10% (8% on most food and drinks). The amount you actually receive may be lower if the shop or its refund operator charges a handling fee. This is a guide, not legal or tax advice — check the official Japan Tourism Agency page and ask the shop.
